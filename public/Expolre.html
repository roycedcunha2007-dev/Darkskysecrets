Stars

  • Massive spheres of plasma held together by gravity
  • Produce energy through nuclear fusion
  • Create heavy elements essential for life
  • Control galaxy evolution
  • The Sun is a yellow dwarf star

Black Holes

  • Extreme gravity traps even light
  • Form from massive star collapse
  • Accretion disks emit radiation
  • Distort spacetime
  • Reside at galaxy centers

Nebulae

  • Birthplaces of stars
  • Composed of gas and dust
  • Glow under stellar radiation
  • Span hundreds of light-years
  • Orion Nebula is visible to naked eye

Galaxies

  • Contain billions of stars
  • Rotate for billions of years
  • Come in spiral & elliptical forms
  • Milky Way has 200+ billion stars
  • Build cosmic structure

Superclusters

  • Largest structures in the universe
  • Contain thousands of galaxies
  • Span millions of light-years
  • Reveal cosmic web
  • Laniakea holds the Milky Way
