Missions Gallery

“Every spacecraft here is a question humanity dared to ask the universe.”

International Space Station

  • Largest structure ever built in orbit
  • Continuously inhabited since 2000
  • Travels at 28,000 km/h
  • Symbol of global cooperation

James Webb Space Telescope

  • Most powerful telescope ever built
  • Infrared observations
  • Studies early galaxies
  • Located at L2 point

Hubble Space Telescope

  • Operating since 1990
  • Revolutionized astronomy
  • Captured iconic deep space images
  • Visible & UV spectrum

Cassini–Huygens

  • Explored Saturn system
  • Discovered subsurface oceans
  • Studied rings & moons
  • Grand Finale in 2017

Voyager 1

  • Farthest human-made object
  • Launched in 1977
  • Entered interstellar space
  • Still transmitting

Parker Solar Probe

  • Closest spacecraft to the Sun
  • Heat shielded
  • Studies solar wind
  • Fastest spacecraft ever

Chandrayaan-2

  • India’s lunar mission
  • Mapped Moon surface
  • Detected water molecules
  • Orbiter still active

Curiosity Rover

  • Car-sized Mars rover
  • Landed in 2012
  • Found evidence of ancient water
  • Still exploring Mars
These missions are more than machines — they are humanity’s curiosity given form. Each one extends our reach deeper into the universe.
