01 / WHY IT MATTERS

83% of humanity lives under a light-polluted sky. One in three of us can no longer see the Milky Way.

  • A

    Sleep

    Artificial light at night suppresses melatonin and disrupts the circadian rhythm every living thing runs on.

  • B

    Wildlife

    Migrating birds and hatchling sea turtles navigate by starlight and moonlight — skyglow sends them the wrong way.

  • C

    Energy

    Light radiated into the sky does nothing for anyone. It is pure waste — billions of dollars of it, every year.

  • D

    The cosmos

    Every civilisation before ours navigated, farmed and dreamed by the night sky. We are the first to lose it.
